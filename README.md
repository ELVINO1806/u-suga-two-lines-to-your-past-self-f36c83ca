A two-line entry for the make.fun bounty: a warning to your past self about one specific moment, and why that moment mattered. The first line names the moment — a phone in hand while a grandmother tells a story that will never be told again — and the second line shows what was lost because of it: an ending heard only once, with no one left who remembers the beginning.

The entry is written plain, with no ornament, because the moment does its own work. The warning is not about a grand life choice but about an ordinary distraction — looking down at a screen during a story that was, in hindsight, the last chance to hear it whole. That small, specific regret is what makes the second line land: the loss is not abstract, it is a beginning nobody alive remembers anymore, and the warning only makes sense because the cause and the effect are the same quiet scene.

## How it works

Running `main.py` writes the two-line entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

```
python3 main.py
```
